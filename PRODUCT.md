# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Spanish-speaking small-business owners across Latin America who need digital marketing, strategy, and a web/software presence but have limited budgets and no in-house team.

## Product Purpose

OhKey Estrategias Digitales (OhKey Digital) helps small businesses grow through budget-conscious digital marketing (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads), strategic consulting, custom software, and live online courses. Success means a client gets a real, working strategy and digital presence without needing enterprise-level spend or a full internal team.

## Positioning

OhKey combines three things most competitors don't:

1. Affordability-first strategy — smart, tailored work scaled to what a small business can actually spend, not generic advice or bargain-bin execution. Tagline: "Con presupuestos pequeños y cerebro" (with small budgets and brains).
2. A genuine all-in-one shop — paid media, strategy, custom software, and live education under one roof, so a client doesn't have to coordinate separate vendors for each.
3. A founder-led, teaching-first brand: a public YouTube channel (youtube.com/@dani.ohkey.digital) signals real expertise being taught in public, not an anonymous agency.

## Operating Context

- Two-person team: "Dani" handles marketing, advertising, and courses; the other co-founder is a software engineer who builds the websites/software.
- The whole pitch lives on a single-page site: slide 1 is the overview/hero with the primary CTA, slide 2 presents the four services as draggable cards that open detail modals.
- Each service's CTA opens a prefilled WhatsApp message (Ecuador country code, +593) rather than a contact form.
- Courses run live online, not pre-recorded.
- Site copy is in Spanish (es locale); no other language is served today.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four services, each with its own WhatsApp CTA:

1. **Pautaje y contenido para redes** — Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads.
2. **Asesoría estratégica** — strategy tailored to the business, not generic advice.
3. **Desarrollo de Software** — websites, apps, AI-driven automation.
4. **Cursos y formación** — live online marketing/advertising/AI courses (no WhatsApp CTA; links to the YouTube channel instead).

## Brand Commitments

- Names in use: "OhKey", "OhKey Estrategias Digitales", "OhKey Digital" — used interchangeably depending on context.
- Existing wordmark logo (inline SVG in the page header, also at `public/logo.svg`): a stylized check/arrow mark in a purple-to-light gradient next to the "OhKey Estrategias Digitales" lettering.
- Brand palette already tokenized in `src/styles/global.css` as `--color-brand-950` → `--color-brand-200` (indigo/violet family), plus a lime-green accent used for emphasis and CTA highlights.
- "Kaushan Script" (Google Fonts) is the committed cursive display face for the word "negocio," chosen specifically as a commercial-use-safe stand-in for an earlier personal-use-only font ("Amsterdam"); do not swap it without reason.
- YouTube channel youtube.com/@dani.ohkey.digital is a standing brand asset (teaching/proof channel), referenced directly from the site.

## Product Principles

1. Speak to limited budgets without sounding cheap — smart and strategic, not bargain-bin.
2. One relationship, many services — reinforce that ads, strategy, software, and courses come from the same accountable two-person team.
3. Teach, don't just sell — the founder-led, education-first voice (YouTube, live courses) is core to trust, not a side channel.
4. Never fabricate proof — no invented testimonials or case studies until real ones exist.
